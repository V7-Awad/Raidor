## Sample workflow

Create a lab entry, attach a topic and environment, then record observations, evidence references, and remediation notes. Entries should stay local by default and should never store real passwords, session cookies, or private tokens.

## Possible entry fields

- Lab name and date.
- Scope and authorization notes.
- Tools and versions used.
- What happened, what was expected, and what was learned.
- Follow-up tasks and links to related write-ups.
