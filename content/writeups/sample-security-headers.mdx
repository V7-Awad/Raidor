## Scope

Use this outline only for a site you own or are explicitly authorized to review. Save the response headers and note the date, environment, and any proxy or CDN in front of the app.

## First pass

Check whether the application has a Content Security Policy that fits its actual resources, a frame policy that prevents unwanted embedding, and an HSTS policy after HTTPS is configured correctly.

Treat scanner output as a starting point. A policy can be present and still be too broad, or conflict with how the application loads scripts and images.

## Follow-up

Record each finding with the affected route, observed header, expected behavior, and a practical fix. Re-check the page after the change and keep the before-and-after responses with your notes.
